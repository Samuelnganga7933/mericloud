How we approach building a system.

Verified client case studies will be published here once we have permission and results we can stand behind. Until then, this is an engineering walk-through of the kind of system we build. It is illustrative and is not a client project.

Illustrative walk-through

A commerce platform for a small retailer

The brief: one system for a shop that sells on a website, social media and a counter, with stock, payments and delivery tracked together.

Operations software in use in a packing area. Illustrative.
The problem

Orders arrive through three channels. Stock is counted separately on each. Payments are matched by hand and delivery status lives in phone calls.

Key decisions
  • One order service for every channel, so a rule is written once
  • Payments confirmed by the provider's callback, never by the customer's browser
  • Stock reserved when payment succeeds and reduced when the parcel is dispatched
  • Every status change recorded with who made it and when
  • Background jobs and retries for anything that depends on a third party
