Questions

Answers before you ask.

Engagement
How do I know which service I need?

You do not have to. Describe what is slow, broken or missing, and we will tell you which part is software, which is process and which is neither. The assessment exists to answer that question before you commit to anything.

Can MeriCloud work with an existing system?

Yes. Most projects involve something that already exists: a spreadsheet, a website, a database, a tool the team likes. We read what is there first, then say what to keep, what to connect and what is cheaper to replace.

Can I approach MeriCloud with just an idea?

Yes. An early idea is the cheapest place to find problems. We help turn it into a clear description of what the system must do, who will use it and what the first useful version looks like.

How does the initial consultation work?

It is a conversation about the problem, the people involved and the tools you use now. We ask a lot of questions. Afterwards we tell you whether we can help and what we would suggest as a next step. There is no obligation to continue.

Delivery
How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, how many systems must connect, how quickly decisions are made and how much data has to move. A focused automation can be much shorter than a platform with payments and delivery. After discovery you get a written plan with phases and estimates, not a guess on the first call.

How are requirements established?

Through discovery: conversations with the people who use the system, a look at the documents and tools involved, and walking through real examples. The result is a written description that you approve before development starts.

Will I be involved during development?

Yes, at set points. You see working releases, try them with real examples and tell us what is wrong. Your time matters most in discovery and when reviewing each release.

How are changes handled?

Changes are normal. Each request is described, its effect on the plan is explained, and you decide whether to include it now, later or not at all.

What happens before a system goes live?

Testing with real data, a check of permissions and backups, a rehearsal of the release and a plan for going back if something goes wrong. Staff are shown how to use it before customers are.

Technology
Can MeriCloud integrate with third-party services?

Usually. Anything that offers an API, a webhook or a data export can be connected: payment providers, couriers, messaging platforms, accounting tools, CRMs. Where a service offers none, we say so early and discuss alternatives.

Can existing data be migrated?

In most cases. We check the quality of the data first, because cleaning it is often the larger part of the job. Migrations are rehearsed on a copy before the real move.

How is security considered during development?

From the start: separate accounts and roles, encrypted connections, safe storage of passwords and keys, logging of important actions and a review of what is exposed. The level of control is matched to the data the system holds.

Can systems be expanded as the business grows?

That is a design aim. We separate the parts of a system so a new channel, branch or integration can be added without rebuilding what already works.

Support
What happens after deployment?

Launch is the start of the system's working life. Support depends on the agreement: monitoring, fixing faults, security updates and small improvements. We settle this before launch, so the question never arises at 3 a.m.

Does MeriCloud provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes, under a defined scope. If you would rather run the system yourselves, we hand it over with documentation and walk your team through it.

Can MeriCloud take over an existing application?

Yes. We begin with an assessment of the code, hosting and accounts, stabilise it, and then agree what ongoing support looks like.

Commercial
How are projects priced?

Price follows scope: how many workflows, how many integrations, how much data to migrate, how critical the system is and how much support it needs. After discovery you receive a written proposal. We do not quote blind.

Is a consultation required before a proposal?

Yes, a short one. A proposal written without understanding the problem is a guess, and guesses are expensive for both sides.

Can projects be delivered in phases?

Yes, and we usually recommend it. A first release that does one thing properly lets you use the system and learn before deciding on the rest.

What should I prepare before contacting MeriCloud?

Nothing formal. It helps to know the problem you want solved, who does the work today, which tools are involved and roughly what a good outcome would be. Examples help: a sample order, a spreadsheet, a screenshot.
