Litigation bundling · Prototype

GoBundle

GoBundle prepares litigation bundles: the paginated, indexed document sets that go before a court or an opposing party.

The problem

Assembling a bundle by hand means ordering hundreds of pages, numbering them, building an index and redoing the work when one document arrives late.

Who it is for

Law firms, in-house legal teams and debt recovery operations that prepare bundles regularly.

Illustrative graphic. A real screenshot of the prototype will replace it.

What it does
  • Add documents to a matter and arrange them into sections
  • Automatic pagination across the whole bundle
  • A generated index that follows the final order
  • Rebuild the bundle when a document is added or moved
  • Export as a single PDF
How it works
1

Add

Documents are added to the matter.

2

Arrange

Sections and order are set.

3

Generate

Pagination and index are built.

4

Review

The bundle is checked and adjusted.

5

Export

One PDF is produced.

Status

Prototype. Not yet available to the public. Capabilities listed describe the prototype's scope.

Questions
Is GoBundle available?

Not yet. It is a prototype. Ask about early access and we will tell you where it stands.

Does it replace our case management system?

No. It is designed to sit beside the tools a firm already uses.

E-signature with an audit trail · Prototype

GoSign

GoSign collects signatures on documents and keeps a record of who signed what, and when.

The problem

Signatures chased by email, scanned and filed are slow to collect and hard to prove later.

Who it is for

Legal teams, recovery operations and small businesses that need signed documents on a regular basis.

Illustrative graphic. A real screenshot of the prototype will replace it.

What it does
  • Send a document to one or more signers
  • Set the signing order
  • Automatic reminders
  • An audit trail for every signer
  • A completed document with a signing record
How it works
1

Upload

The document is added.

2

Add signers

Names, contacts and order are set.

3

Send

Signers receive a secure link.

4

Track

You see who has signed.

5

Complete

The signed document and record are stored.

Status

Prototype. Not yet available to the public. Capabilities listed describe the prototype's scope.

Questions
Is GoSign available?

Not yet. It is a prototype.

Will it connect to GoBundle?

That is the intention: signed documents going straight into a matter's bundle.
