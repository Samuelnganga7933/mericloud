When you would need it

Teams drowning in repetitive messages, documents or enquiries, and businesses that want AI features without handing control to a black box.

The problems it solves

  • Staff retype or re-read the same kinds of messages all day
  • A chatbot trial gave confident wrong answers
  • Nobody can say what the AI did, or why
How it works

A reliable AI feature is mostly engineering around the model. The model is one part of the system.

1

Input

Email, chat, forms or documents arrive from the channels you already use.

2

Context

Relevant policies, products or records are retrieved so answers come from your facts.

3

Model

A language model classifies, extracts or drafts under strict instructions.

4

Checks

Confidence thresholds and rules decide what may be sent automatically.

5

Human review

Anything uncertain goes to a person with the draft ready to approve.

6

Log

Every decision is recorded so quality can be measured and improved.

How we build it
STEP 1Pick one job

A narrow task with a clear definition of a good answer.

STEP 2Build a test set

Real examples become an evaluation set, so quality is measured, not guessed.

STEP 3Wire in guardrails

Limits, review steps and logging before anything reaches a customer.

STEP 4Launch and tune

Run beside staff first, then widen what it handles as results hold.

What you receive
  • A working assistant or agent in your channels
  • An evaluation set and quality report
  • A review queue for uncertain cases
  • Logs of every automated decision
  • Documentation of its limits and how to change them

Typical technology: LLM APIs, Python, vector search, n8n

The outcome

Fewer repetitive messages for your staff, and every automated answer traceable and open to review.

It starts with an assessment: a conversation and a read of what exists, ending in a written plan you can accept or leave. Work then proceeds in phases you approve, and support continues as agreed.

Questions we get
Will it make things up?

It can, which is why answers are grounded in your documents, checked against rules and routed to a person when confidence is low.

Where does our data go?

We choose the model provider with you and keep sensitive data out of prompts where possible. Data handling is agreed in writing.

Can it take actions, not just answer?

Yes, within permissions you set, such as creating a ticket or updating a record, with every action logged.
