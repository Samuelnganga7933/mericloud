How it works

Most business systems are the same few parts, built on purpose.

Whether it sells products, tracks legal cases or answers customers, a system like this has five layers. Knowing them makes it easier to judge any quote, including ours.

Where people use itWebsiteMobile appStaff dashboardPoint of saleWhatsApp and email
The coreAPIBusiness rulesPermissionsBackground jobs
What it remembersDatabaseFile storageAudit history
What it connects toPaymentsCouriersSMS and emailAccountingAI models
What keeps it standingCloud hostingNetwork and VPNMonitoring and alertsBackupsSecurity controls

One core, many doors

Your website, app and staff screens all talk to the same core. A price or stock change happens once and shows up everywhere.

Connections, not copy-paste

Payments, couriers, messaging and accounting are linked through APIs, so information moves by itself and every movement is recorded.

Built to stay up

Hosting, backups, monitoring and access control are designed in from the start, because that is what you rely on at 3 a.m.

Questions

What people ask before they get in touch.

How do I know which service I need?

You do not have to. Describe what is slow, broken or missing, and we will tell you which part is software, which is process and which is neither. The assessment exists to answer that question before you commit to anything.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, how many systems must connect, how quickly decisions are made and how much data has to move. A focused automation can be much shorter than a platform with payments and delivery. After discovery you get a written plan with phases and estimates, not a guess on the first call.

Can MeriCloud integrate with third-party services?

Usually. Anything that offers an API, a webhook or a data export can be connected: payment providers, couriers, messaging platforms, accounting tools, CRMs. Where a service offers none, we say so early and discuss alternatives.

Can MeriCloud take over an existing application?

Yes. We begin with an assessment of the code, hosting and accounts, stabilise it, and then agree what ongoing support looks like.

How are projects priced?

Price follows scope: how many workflows, how many integrations, how much data to migrate, how critical the system is and how much support it needs. After discovery you receive a written proposal. We do not quote blind.

What should I prepare before contacting MeriCloud?

Nothing formal. It helps to know the problem you want solved, who does the work today, which tools are involved and roughly what a good outcome would be. Examples help: a sample order, a spreadsheet, a screenshot.
