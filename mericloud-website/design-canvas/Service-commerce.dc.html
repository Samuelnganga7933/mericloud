The tracking systems we build
Order tracking

Every order gets one number, wherever it came from.

A website checkout, a WhatsApp message and a walk-in sale land in the same order list with the same statuses: new, paid, picking, packed, out for delivery, delivered. The owner sees what is waiting. The packer sees what to pick. Nobody has to ask whether that one went out.

  • One order list fed by the website, social channels and the shop counter
  • A status workflow with a timestamp and a name on every change
  • Staff roles, so packers pack and only managers refund
  • Order history and lookup by customer, phone number or order number
Stock tracking

Counts that match the shelf.

When an order is paid, the items are reserved so the last unit is not sold twice. When it ships, the count drops. When something runs low, a reorder list builds itself. A barcode scan at receiving and packing keeps the numbers honest, and a stock-take becomes a check rather than a rebuild.

  • Live stock by product, variant and location
  • Reservations that prevent overselling
  • Low-stock alerts and reorder lists
  • Barcode scanning for receiving, picking and stock-takes
  • Sync between web, social and point of sale
Delivery tracking

Know where every parcel is, and tell the customer before they ask.

An order is assigned to a courier or rider with the address, the phone number and the amount to collect. Updates come back from the courier system or the rider's phone: picked up, on the way, delivered, failed. The customer gets a tracking link. Cash collected on delivery is recorded and settled at the end of the day.

  • Courier and rider integrations, with delivery zones and fees
  • Tracking links and SMS or WhatsApp updates
  • Proof of delivery by photo, signature or one-time code
  • Cash-on-delivery recording and settlement
  • Failed-delivery and returns handling
Money tracking

Every payment matched to its order.

Card, bank, wallet and cash payments are recorded against the order they belong to. Failed and duplicate payments are flagged. Refunds are tracked. At the end of the day the owner sees takings by channel, and the accountant receives an export instead of a shoebox of receipts.

  • Card, bank and wallet payments through your provider's gateway, confirmed by its callback
  • Reconciliation of takings by channel and by day
  • Refunds and partial payments, recorded properly
  • Margin reporting and export to accounting tools
The outcome

One record of every order, payment, parcel and unit of stock, so the owner stops reconciling and starts deciding.

It starts with an assessment: a conversation and a read of what exists, ending in a written plan you can accept or leave. Work then proceeds in phases you approve, and support continues as agreed.

Questions we get
Can you connect to our payment provider and courier?

Usually, yes. Both normally offer APIs or callbacks. We confirm what each provider supports early in the project.

Can we keep selling on our current site while you build?

Yes. We build and test alongside it, then switch over in stages.

Do we need a physical shop for this?

No. The same system works for online-only, in-store only or both.
