When you would need it

Businesses handling customer, legal or financial data, teams with shared passwords and unreviewed admin rights, and anyone facing a customer security questionnaire.

The problems it solves

  • Former staff or shared accounts still have access
  • Nobody can say who changed or deleted a record
  • A breach would mean guessing what was affected
How it works

Security is layers of control, each assuming the one before it might fail.

1

Identity

Every person has their own account with multi-factor sign-in.

2

Access

People get only the permissions their job needs, reviewed regularly.

3

Hardening

Servers and applications are configured to close unnecessary doors.

4

Secrets and data

Keys and passwords are stored safely and sensitive data is encrypted.

5

Audit and alerts

Sign-ins and changes are logged, and unusual activity raises an alert.

6

Response

A written plan for what to do, who to call and how to restore.

How we build it
STEP 1Review

List assets, accounts and access, and find what is exposed.

STEP 2Prioritise

Fixes ranked by risk and effort, in plain language.

STEP 3Fix and harden

Access, configuration and monitoring changes made and verified.

STEP 4Rehearse

Test the response plan so it works under pressure.

What you receive
  • An access review with a prioritised findings report
  • Multi-factor sign-in and role-based access
  • Hardened servers and applications
  • Audit logging and alert rules
  • An incident response and recovery plan

Typical technology: Identity and access management, MFA, logging, secure development

The outcome

Known access, visible activity and a rehearsed response, matched to the data you hold.

It starts with an assessment: a conversation and a read of what exists, ending in a written plan you can accept or leave. Work then proceeds in phases you approve, and support continues as agreed.
