About

An engineering company that starts with your operations.

MeriCloud is a remote-first software and systems engineering company. We build applications, integrations, automation and infrastructure, and we keep them running.

We think a system is only as good as the fit between the software and the people who use it. So the first job is understanding the business, and the second is engineering it properly.

Engineers working through a design together.

The business side

Operations, customers, workflows and the commercial reason for the project. We ask who does the work, what goes wrong and what a good result is worth.

The technical side

Software, integration, automation, hosting, networks and security. We choose technology for the problem, explain the trade-offs and document what we build.

Where they meet

Decisions that look technical, such as which database or which provider, often turn on a business fact. We keep both in the room.

How we work

Fix before replace

We improve what works before proposing a rewrite.

Small releases

Working pieces you can try beat a big reveal after months of silence.

Design for failure

Backups, alerts and recovery are part of the first release.

Leave it readable

Clear code and documentation, so nobody is locked in to us.

What we will not claim

We do not publish clients, testimonials, certifications or statistics until we can show them. When evidence is missing from this site it is because it is not yet ours to show.
