When you would need it

Law firms, paralegal teams and debt recovery operations that assemble court bundles by hand, chase signatures by email and track deadlines in spreadsheets.

The problems it solves

  • Bundles take hours of manual pagination and indexing
  • Signatures arrive late and are hard to prove
  • Deadlines live in someone's calendar

Questions we get
Do you replace our case management system?

Not necessarily. We can connect to it or build the missing pieces beside it.

Is client data secure?

Access is role-based, activity is logged, and data handling is agreed in writing.

Can it handle debt recovery volumes?

It is designed around repeatable matter types, which is how recovery teams work.
