# Website brief (summary)

**Company:** software and systems engineering. Builds, connects and maintains custom software, AI, automation, cloud and security, plus legal technology.

**Goal:** a commercial website that converts prospective clients: explain what the company does, show how systems work and how they are built, answer objections, and make contact easy.

**Pages:** Home, Services (with a page per field), Products, Work, Process, About, Insights, Contact, plus Security, Careers and legal pages.

**Look:** navy and orange; light by default, dark by following the browser setting; editorial layouts; real photography and software screenshots rather than decorative graphics; a scroll-driven zoom-out in the hero.

**Writing rules:** specific and plain; no slogans or filler; no invented clients, testimonials, statistics, prices or timelines. Anything not yet real is labelled illustrative, internal or prototype.

**Not aimed at a single local market.** Concrete services are listed (for example AI agents, AI orchestration) instead of business-model descriptions.
